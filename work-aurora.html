Work / Aurora

Rebuilding Aurora into a fintech brand people actually trust.

Client
Aurora Financial
Services
Brand, Web, Product
Timeline
14 weeks
Year
2025

The challenge

Aurora had strong fundamentals — a fast-growing lending product and loyal early customers — but a brand and site that looked like a hundred other fintech startups. Conversion on their marketing site sat at 1.1%, well below category benchmarks, and their product felt clinical rather than confident.

What we did

We started with two weeks of stakeholder interviews and competitive teardown before touching a single pixel. From there, we rebuilt Aurora's positioning around "confident finance," developed a bold new visual identity anchored in a custom typeface pairing and a warm-cool gradient system, then redesigned and rebuilt their marketing site and onboarding flow in Next.js.

"Working with Pulsar felt like adding three senior hires to our team overnight — not hiring an outside agency."
