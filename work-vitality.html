Work / Vitality

A health app onboarding built to feel like a personal trainer, not a form.

Client
Vitality Health
Services
Product, UX Research
Timeline
11 weeks
Year
2025

The challenge

Vitality's clinical assessment app was medically excellent but users abandoned onboarding before finishing it — 58% dropped off before completing their first health check-in, largely because the flow read like an insurance form rather than a conversation.

What we did

We ran contextual interviews with 24 patients across three age groups, then rebuilt the entire intake flow around adaptive micro-steps — asking only what's relevant, one decision at a time, with a visible sense of progress. We paired this with a warmer visual language: rounded typography, a calming palette and illustration system replacing clinical iconography.

"Our completion rate didn't just improve — patients started telling us the onboarding felt like it actually cared."
