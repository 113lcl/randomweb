FAQ

Questions, answered.

How fast can we start?

Most engagements kick off within two weeks of signing. Sprint projects can sometimes start sooner if our calendar allows.

Do you work with early-stage startups?

Yes — about a third of our roster is pre-seed to Series A. We offer flexible scoping for teams still finding product-market fit.

What tools do you design and build in?

Figma for design, and React/Next.js or Webflow for build depending on your team's technical needs and maintenance plans.

Can we hire you for ongoing support?

Absolutely — our Partnership model is built exactly for that, with month-to-month flexibility and no long-term lock-in.
