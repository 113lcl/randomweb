Studio / About us

Nine years of turning ambitious briefs into brands people trust.

Pulsar started in a Warsaw apartment in 2016 with two laptops and one client. Today we're a 14-person studio working with founders and marketing teams across three continents — but the obsession with craft hasn't changed.

Our approach

We treat every brief like it's the only one that matters.

No account managers relaying messages, no junior team quietly doing the work. You get direct access to the people actually designing and building your product — from kickoff to launch and beyond.

We keep our roster deliberately small. It means we say no to a lot of good projects so we can say yes to a few great ones, fully.

What we believe

Craft over volume

We'd rather ship fewer projects at a higher bar than spread thin chasing quantity.

Speed with intent

Fast doesn't mean rushed. We move quickly by cutting meetings, not corners.

Radical transparency

Shared timelines, honest budgets, and direct feedback — even when it's uncomfortable.

Built to be loved

Metrics matter, but we design for the moment a user smiles at a small detail.

Our story

Nine years,
one studio.

2016
Two founders, one apartment

Pulsar launches with a single freelance branding project and a shared belief that small teams do better work.

2018
First product engagement

We ship our first full product design + build for a Berlin fintech startup, expanding beyond pure branding.

2020
Studio goes remote-first

We restructure around a distributed team, hiring senior talent across five countries.

2022
100th project shipped

We cross our 100th shipped engagement and open a small studio space in Warsaw for the core team.

2026
Fourteen people, one standard

Today the team spans strategy, design and engineering — still holding the same bar as day one.
