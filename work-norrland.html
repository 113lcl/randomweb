Work / Norrland

A storefront that finally sells the way Norrland's gear deserves.

Client
Norrland Outdoor
Services
Web Design, Development
Timeline
9 weeks
Year
2024

The challenge

Norrland makes some of the best cold-weather outdoor gear in Scandinavia, but their storefront — a bloated theme with six checkout steps — buried that quality under friction. Mobile checkout abandonment sat above 80%, well past category norms.

What we did

We rebuilt the storefront from scratch on a headless commerce stack, cutting checkout to a single scrollable page and rebuilding product pages around large-format photography and honest material storytelling. Every animation was tuned to feel tactile — like handling the product itself — without adding a single millisecond to load time.

"We expected a redesign. We got a storefront that finally sells the way our products deserve."
